Few lines on a restaurant bill cause as many arguments as "Service charge 10%". Guests feel it's forced on them; owners see it as a way to reward staff. Since 2022 there are clear national guidelines on it, and getting it wrong can lead to complaints against your café.

This guide explains the rules in plain language, so your bills stay fair, clear and complaint-free.

> **The short answer:** under the Central Consumer Protection Authority's 2022 guidelines, a café or restaurant can't add service charge to the bill automatically or by default, can't force guests to pay it, and can't collect it under another name. Tips should be voluntary and separate from the bill. This guide is general information, not legal advice; check the latest position with your CA or lawyer.

## What the CCPA guidelines say

In July 2022, the Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) issued guidelines to stop unfair practices around service charge in hotels and restaurants. In short:

- **No automatic service charge.** It can't be added to the bill by default.
- **No other name.** It can't be collected as a "staff fee", "service fee" or anything similar.
- **No compulsion.** A guest can't be asked to pay it, and entry or service can't depend on it.
- **Tell guests clearly** that any service charge or tip is voluntary and at their discretion.
- **No GST on top of a service charge** added to the food bill, since it isn't supposed to be on the bill in the first place.

Restaurant associations challenged these guidelines in the Delhi High Court. In March 2025, the court upheld them, saying that forcing guests to pay a service charge is an unfair trade practice. The restaurant associations have appealed to a Division Bench of the same court, so check the current status before you rely on any one reading.

## What guests can do if they're charged

Guests who are charged a service charge against these guidelines can ask for it to be removed, and can complain through the National Consumer Helpline (1915), the consumer commission or the district collector. A complaint is a headache no café needs, especially when it ends up in reviews.

## Service charge vs GST vs tip

| | What it is | On the bill? |
|---|---|---|
| **GST** | Tax charged on your food and service, passed to the government | Yes, by law, shown as CGST and SGST |
| **Service charge** | An extra percentage added by the restaurant | Should not be added automatically |
| **Tip** | Money a guest chooses to give staff | Separate from the bill, fully voluntary |

For most standalone cafés and restaurants, GST is 5% without input tax credit, split equally into CGST and SGST. Our guide to [GST on restaurant bills](/blog/gst-on-restaurant-bills-india/) explains it in detail.

## What a fair, complaint-proof bill looks like

- **Menu prices and taxes are clear.** Guests can see whether prices include GST or GST is added.
- **Only GST is added on top.** No automatic service charge, packing charge for dine-in or vague "other charges".
- **Discounts are shown openly**, with the amount, so guests see exactly what they saved.
- **Tips, if any, are separate.** A tip jar at the counter, or staff accepting a tip the guest offers, not a line on the bill.

## How to reward staff without a service charge

Service charge was often a way to share money with staff. You can still do that fairly:

1. **Build it into your prices.** If you need ₹15,000 a month more for staff, a few rupees on high-selling items covers it, and guests never see a surprise line.
2. **Welcome voluntary tips.** A visible tip jar or a "tips for the team" QR at the counter. Many guests tip when service was good and nobody forced them.
3. **Share targets, not percentages.** A monthly bonus when the café hits a sales or review goal motivates the whole team.
4. **Praise in public.** Mention staff by name when replying to good Google reviews. It costs nothing and people remember it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is service charge mandatory in restaurants in India?

No. Under the CCPA's 2022 guidelines, a restaurant can't add service charge automatically or make guests pay it. Any service charge or tip has to be voluntary. The Delhi High Court upheld these guidelines in March 2025.

### Can a restaurant add service charge if it's written on the menu?

Writing it on the menu doesn't make it compulsory. The guidelines say it can't be added by default or collected under any other name. Check the latest legal position with your CA or lawyer before charging anything beyond GST.

### Is GST charged on service charge?

The guidelines say service charge shouldn't be added to the bill and GST shouldn't be levied on it as part of the total. GST applies to the food and service you sell, at the rate that applies to your restaurant.

### What can a customer do if a restaurant charges service charge?

They can ask for it to be removed from the bill, and if the restaurant refuses, complain through the National Consumer Helpline on 1915, the consumer commission or the district collector.

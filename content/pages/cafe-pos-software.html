Café POS software: questions owners ask

What is the best POS software for a small café in India?

Look for correct GST bills, UPI, cash and card on one bill, a reliable way to send orders to the kitchen, billing that works offline and support for the devices you already own. Mezana covers all of these and is built for dine-in cafés.

Do I need a POS machine for my café?

No. Mezana runs in the browser on an Android tablet, iPad, laptop or phone. Most cafés only add a thermal printer for bills and kitchen slips.

Can guests order from their table?

Yes. Each table has its own QR card. Guests scan it with their phone camera, order without downloading an app and pay at the counter as usual.

How long does setup take?

We set up your menu, print the table QR cards and train your staff. You go live when you’re ready, and your 30 free days start then.

Questions owners ask

What happens after the 30 free days?

If Mezana is helping your café, you continue at ₹2,000 a month. If not, you stop. There is no contract and nothing to return.

Do I need to buy new hardware?

No. Mezana runs in the browser on the phones, tablets and laptops you already have. For printed bills, use a 58 mm or 80 mm thermal printer connected to your counter device.

What if the internet goes down?

The counter keeps working on its device. You can take orders, print kitchen slips and bills and take payment, with real invoice numbers. When the internet is back, everything is sent in order.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. They scan the QR card on their table and the menu opens in their phone’s browser. They pay at the counter as they do today.

Why only 10 founding cafés?

We set up every founding café ourselves and stay close while you settle in. Ten is how many we can look after properly.

Is my café’s data safe?

Each café’s orders, bills and guest list are walled off from every other café by the database itself, and each login belongs to one café only.

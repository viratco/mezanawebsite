KOT software: questions owners ask

What is KOT software?

KOT software creates kitchen order tickets automatically when an order is taken and sends them to the kitchen, on a screen or a printer, so nothing is handwritten or lost.

What is a kitchen display system (KDS)?

A kitchen display system shows orders on a screen in the kitchen instead of paper slips, with timing, notes and a way to mark dishes ready.

Do I need a KOT printer?

Not necessarily. Mezana’s kitchen screen runs on any tablet. Many kitchens keep a small thermal printer as well for reprints.

Does the kitchen screen work with QR orders?

Yes. QR orders from guests reach the kitchen screen as soon as staff accept them at the counter.

How QR code ordering works

  1. Guest scans the table QRThe menu opens in the phone’s browser, already linked to that table.
  2. Guest ordersPhotos, veg-only filter, sizes, options and add-ons. The guest can add their name and number if they like.
  3. Counter acceptsThe order rings at the counter. One tap accepts it, which stops prank orders and lets you catch anything unusual.
  4. Kitchen cooksThe order appears on the kitchen screen with the table, quantities and notes. Staff are told when it’s ready.
  5. One running billEverything the table ordered collects on one bill, paid at the counter by UPI, cash or card.

QR code ordering: questions owners ask

Do guests need to download an app to order by QR code?

No. The QR code opens your menu in the phone’s browser straight from the camera.

Do guests pay on their phone?

No. Guests order from their phone and pay at the counter as they do today, by UPI, cash, card or a mix of these.

Can waiters still take orders?

Yes. Staff can add orders to the same table bill, so guests who prefer to order from a person can.

What if someone orders as a joke?

Orders only reach the kitchen after staff accept them at the counter.

How do I get the QR codes for my tables?

Mezana prints a QR card for every table, with your café’s name. Put one on each table and you’re ready.

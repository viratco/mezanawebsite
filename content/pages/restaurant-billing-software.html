Restaurant billing software: questions owners ask

Is Mezana GST billing software for restaurants?

Yes. Mezana prints GST tax invoices with your GSTIN and FSSAI number, CGST and SGST shown separately, the SAC code for restaurant service and an invoice number series for each financial year.

Can I bill without internet?

Yes. The counter keeps working on its device: take orders, print kitchen slips and bills, and take payment, with real invoice numbers. When the internet is back, everything is sent in order and nothing is doubled.

Which printer do I need?

Any 58 mm or 80 mm thermal printer connected to your counter device. You don’t need to buy a new computer or a POS machine; Mezana runs in the browser.

Can guests split the bill between UPI and cash?

Yes. One bill can be paid partly by UPI, partly by cash and partly by card, and the day’s closing shows each separately.

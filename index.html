Waiting for the internet…

  • Opens with no internet. The counter page and your menu are kept on the device.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Mezana only for cafés, or for restaurants too?

Both. Mezana is dine-in software, made for any place where guests sit at tables: cafés, restaurants, bistros, bakeries and dessert shops. It covers table ordering, the kitchen, billing and payments in one system.

Do I need to buy new hardware?

No. Mezana runs in the browser on the phones, tablets and laptops you already have. For printed bills, use a 58 mm or 80 mm thermal printer connected to your counter device.

What happens when the internet goes down?

The counter keeps working on its device. You can add dishes, accept QR orders already on screen, print kitchen slips and bills, and take payment. Bills keep real invoice numbers. When the internet is back, everything is sent in order, and nothing is ever doubled.

Do guests have to download an app?

No. They scan the QR card on their table and the menu opens in their phone’s browser.

Do guests pay on their phone?

No. Guests order from their phone and pay at the counter as they do today, by UPI, cash, card or a mix of these.

Can I change my menu and prices myself?

Yes. From the Menu page you can add dishes, photos, categories, sizes, options and add-ons, and change prices. The next guest who scans sees the new menu.

Are the bills GST-ready?

Yes. Mezana prints tax invoices with your GSTIN and FSSAI number, CGST and SGST shown separately, and an invoice number series for each financial year.

Is my café’s data safe?

Each café’s orders, bills and guest list are walled off from every other café by the database itself, and each login belongs to one café only. Prices are always taken from your menu on our server, never from the guest’s phone.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of your café. Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll give you a straight answer, along with your free demo.
